## Draft 1: SDG India Index

**UNVERIFIED — EDITOR REVIEW REQUIRED**

- **Proposed title:** SDG India Index 2023-24: How the Index Measures State and Union Territory Progress
- **Proposed summary:** NITI Aayog's fourth SDG India Index edition assesses progress across 16 Sustainable Development Goals for India's States and Union Territories. It combines selected official indicators into goal-level and composite scores; its indicator set and mixed reference periods mean scores need to be read alongside the report's methodology and data notes.
- **Reporting period / edition:** Edition 2023-24. It is not a single-year dataset: the report uses the latest available observations, with different reference periods by indicator. NITI says over 80% of indicators draw on the last three years, while some use data from 2015-16 to 2020-21. Record each indicator's own stated period, not a blanket 2023-24 value year.
- **Publisher/source organization:** NITI Aayog, Government of India.
- **Official source URL:** [Original report PDF](https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-07/SDA_INDIA_Publication.pdf) · [NITI publication record](https://www.niti.gov.in/node/1350) · [NITI SDG division report listing](https://www.niti.gov.in/publications/division-reports?page=11)
- **Proposed category:** State & District Data (also tag Government & Official Reports; do not imply district-level scores are in this index).
- **Methodology notes:** The report describes 113 indicators across 70 targets and 16 scored SDGs. It says 107 indicators were used for index estimation; five Goal 14 indicators were excluded because they cover only nine coastal States, and one Goal 10 indicator was excluded for lack of comparability. Indicator values are normalized to a 0-100 scale using direction-specific formulas, then averaged for goal scores; the composite is formed from the goal scores. See report Sections 1-4, approximately pp. 58-59, Sections 5-7, approximately pp. 59-60, and “Reading the Index 2023-24,” p. 61. Confirm page numbering against the downloaded PDF.
- **Supported fact for a possible key finding:** The report states that some State/UT indicator cells are null where data is unavailable; nulls are omitted from the relevant score calculation rather than treated as zero. See “Summary of Indicators,” approximately p. 58.
- **Limitations/context to display:** This edition is not strictly comparable with prior editions because the indicator set was revised. Reference years vary; some indicators are older than others. Sources are largely Union ministry surveys, official portals and scheme MIS; the report notes concerns that State-only schemes and State-provided data may not be fully captured. Missing values and the coastal-only Goal 14 exclusions affect coverage. The index is a defined progress-tracking tool, not a complete measure of SDG outcomes.
- **Editor checks before verification:** Confirm the PDF is the final version; verify exact page numbers; enter the edition's indicator-level reference periods only from its tables; decide whether to publish the report record without separately importing scores; do not construct new rankings from these notes.

## Draft 2: PLFS Annual Report 2025

**UNVERIFIED — EDITOR REVIEW REQUIRED**

- **Proposed title:** Periodic Labour Force Survey Annual Report 2025: Employment Indicators for India
- **Proposed summary:** The National Statistics Office's PLFS Annual Report 2025 presents employment and unemployment estimates for rural and urban India for the calendar year January-December 2025. It reports indicators including labour force participation, worker-population ratio and unemployment under both usual-status and current-weekly-status concepts. The 2025 sample design changed, so comparisons with earlier annual reports require care.
- **Reporting period:** January-December 2025 (calendar year; the report's first comprehensive annual cycle under this schedule).
- **Publisher/source organization:** National Statistics Office (NSO), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India.
- **Official source URL:** [Original report PDF](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/publications_reports/publications_reports1780040415321_0624fb13-fb47-40bc-b470-7c7e9635c3ef_PLFS_2025_F_REV_29052026.pdf) · [Official PIB report release and methodology note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246009&lang=1&reg=1) · [MoSPI report portal](https://www.mospi.gov.in/download-reports?ma=)
- **Publication date:** PIB records 27 March 2026 at 4:00 PM for the report release. Keep this separate from the January-December 2025 survey period.
- **Proposed category:** Economy & Employment (also tag Government & Official Reports).
- **Methodology notes:** PLFS is a household survey. From January 2025 the design uses four consecutive monthly visits in a rotational panel for rural and urban areas; district is the basic stratum separately for rural/urban sectors across most covered geography, with NSS regions used for remaining areas. Key status concepts differ by reference period: usual status considers the preceding 365 days; current weekly status (CWS) considers the preceding seven days. See report Chapter 1, especially Section 1.5 and Table 2 (report pp. 5-6); indicator definitions are in the report's conceptual-framework section and PIB endnote.
- **Supported fact for a possible key finding:** The report release states that 22,594 of 22,692 allocated first-stage units were surveyed, covering 270,472 households and 1,148,634 people. Cite the sample-size section (report/PIB endnote, section B); do not present the sample as a census.
- **Limitations/context to display:** MoSPI explicitly cautions that 2025 annual estimates may not be strictly comparable with prior PLFS annual reports or 2022-24 calendar-year estimates because sampling design and estimation procedures changed. Results are estimates from a sample, not complete counts. Use the report's own definitions and distinguish usual status from CWS; do not label these as district-level estimates merely because districts form strata.
- **Editor checks before verification:** Confirm the final PDF revision and publication metadata; check any specific indicator/table and population denominator before writing a claim; preserve the survey's status definition, age group, rural/urban split and units in every data record.

## Draft 3: UNDP Human Development Report 2025

**UNVERIFIED — EDITOR REVIEW REQUIRED**

- **Proposed title:** Human Development Report 2025: India’s HDI and the Global Human Development Picture
- **Proposed summary:** UNDP's 2025 Human Development Report examines human development and choices in the age of artificial intelligence. Its statistical annex reports country-level HDI values and component measures. For India, the report's 2023 observation is an HDI value of 0.685 and rank 130; these are the report's 2025-edition figures, not a live or current-year measure.
- **Reporting period / reference years:** Edition published 6 May 2025. The India HDI table labels HDI rank, HDI value and most components as 2023; the GNI-per-capita-rank-minus-HDI-rank column is labeled 2022. Do not treat publication year as the data year.
- **Publisher/source organization:** United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Human Development Report Office.
- **Official source URL:** [Official report page](https://hdr.undp.org/content/human-development-report-2025) · [English report PDF](https://hdr.undp.org/system/files/documents/global-report-document/hdr2025reporten.pdf) · [India release](https://www.undp.org/india/press-releases/indias-human-development-continues-make-progress-ranks-130-out-193-countries) · [2025 technical notes](https://hdr.undp.org/sites/default/files/2025_HDR/hdr2025_technical_notes.pdf) · [Errata and corrigenda](https://hdr.undp.org/errata-and-corrigenda-hdr-2025)
- **Proposed category:** India in the World (also tag Human Development and Government & Official Reports).
- **Methodology notes:** HDI summarizes health, education and standard-of-living dimensions. UNDP normalizes component indicators using stated goalposts; education combines expected and mean years of schooling, income is transformed logarithmically, and the three dimension indices are combined using a geometric mean. See Technical Note 1, report pp. 2-3 (PDF pages 2-3). India is listed at rank 130 with HDI 0.685 in Table 1, report printed p. 275 (PDF page 289); component values in the same row are life expectancy 72.0 years, expected schooling 13.0 years, mean schooling 6.9 years and GNI per capita 9,047 in 2021 PPP dollars.
- **Limitations/context to display:** HDI captures only part of human development and does not itself measure inequality, poverty, empowerment or human security; UNDP directs readers to other measures for these dimensions. The report states that HDRO revises the historical time series with each new HDR as source data and goalposts are updated. The report's general disclaimer says analytical estimates may not be national official statistics, while the statistical annex figures are from official sources. UNDP has published errata for this edition; use the current corrected file and disclose the cited table/page.
- **Editor checks before verification:** Check the errata page and current PDF before copying any figures; retain 2023 as the observation year; distinguish India’s rank/value from the report's broader AI analysis; do not describe the report as an official Indian government assessment.
